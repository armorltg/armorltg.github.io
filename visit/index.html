1 Park Map & Route
🟢 Route Key Points
📌 Start: Park entrance on Baojiang Road (West Gate)
🏢 End: Building 10, Room 302
🅿️ Parking: Right side after the entrance barrier
🚗 Route: ~200m straight along the main road
2 Step-by-Step Directions
1

🚗 Drive to the Park Entrance on Baojiang Road

Search "Shenlan Industrial Park" in your navigation app. As you arrive on Baojiang Road, look for the blue park information board on the right. The entrance has a boom barrier and guard booth.

⏱ Travel time depends on your starting point

2

🚪 Enter the Park (West Gate / Barrier)

Pass through the boom barrier into the park. If registration is required, please check in at the guard booth. Buildings on both sides have white facades with blue accent lines.

⏱ ~1 minute

3

➡️ Continue Straight Along the Main Road (~200m)

After entering, head straight along the main road. You will pass factory buildings on both sides, with greenery lining the road.

⏱ ~2–3 minutes on foot

4

🏢 Arrive at Building 10

Look for Building 10 — a white building with blue accent lines. The ARMOR logo (blue-green crown icon) is displayed at the ground-floor entrance. Suite number: 10#302.

⏱ You've arrived

5

✅ Welcome to ARMOR

Go upstairs to Room 302 — this is the ARMOR Huamang Optoelectronics office. Please confirm your arrival time with your contact person in advance.

⏱ ~1 minute on foot

4 Visitor Notes
🅿️

Parking

After passing the barrier, parking is available on the right side. If full, continue along the internal roads for additional spots.

📋

Park Registration

A guard may be on duty and require visitor registration. We recommend informing your contact person of your arrival time so we can meet you at the gate.

⏰

Office Hours

Weekdays 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Please call your contact person 10 minutes before arrival to ensure someone is available to welcome you.

🧭

Navigation Tip

Search "Shenlan Industrial Park" (深蓝工业园) in Gaode Maps or Baidu Maps, and set the destination to the West Gate on Baojiang Road.
